Game 4 - Bonus Game

X O Tic-Tac-Toe

Two-player local showdown. Connect three marks in a line to triumph!

Rules and Regulations

Objective: Place three of your marks in a row.

Rules

  1. Two players take turns, Player 1 as X and Player 2 as O. X always starts.
  2. Click an empty square to place your mark.
  3. The first player to get 3 marks in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) wins.
  4. If all 9 squares are filled and nobody has won, the game is a draw.

Regulations

  • A square that is already filled cannot be changed.
  • Players must alternate turns.
  • Once someone wins, the board is locked until "New Game" is clicked.
