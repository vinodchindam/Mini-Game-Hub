Game 2 - Easy

✊ ✋ ✌ Rock-Paper-Scissors

Take on the computer in the classic duel of hands. First to 5 wins!

Rules and Regulations

Objective: Beat the computer in each round.

Rules

  1. Choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors. The computer picks one at random.
  2. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, and Paper beats Rock.
  3. If both choose the same, the round is a draw.
  4. The first player to reach 5 points wins the match.
  5. A win gives 1 point; a draw gives no points.

Regulations

  • Only one choice per round.
  • The computer's choice is hidden until you choose.
  • After the match ends, buttons are disabled until Restart is clicked.
